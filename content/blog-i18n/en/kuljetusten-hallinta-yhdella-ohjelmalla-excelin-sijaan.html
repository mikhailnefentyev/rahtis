Moving from Excel to a single program can be done without disruption if you do it in stages: move customers, vehicles and open invoices in order, run the new and the old side by side for two billing periods, and stop using Excel only once invoicing and proofs of delivery work in the program without discrepancies. Managing transport in one program instead of Excel means that the order, the transport, the consignment note and the invoice are in a single chain from start to finish. This guide explains what data to move, in what order, and which mistakes derail the switch in a small firm.

Why is Excel no longer enough once you have more than one vehicle?

Excel fails because nobody has time to keep it up to date. When you are the dispatcher, the invoicing clerk and the safety manager all at once, the spreadsheet gets updated in the evening, if at all. The same happens with freight exchanges: separate logins to TIMOCOM, Teleroute and Trans.eu mean browsing loads in three places, and a free vehicle easily ends up without a backload.

A typical situation in a small haulage firm: separate exchange logins, an old program and a paper notebook recording who owes whom. Invoices, proofs of delivery and order confirmations are in different files, and payment tracking relies on memory. When the same information is entered at least twice, errors multiply into every report.

A single program solves this by chaining the data together. RAHTIS – transport management and freight exchange: semi-trailers, containers and express transport is built for this: order, run, consignment note and invoice are in one place, and the driver confirms each stage in the app.

What data should you move from Excel, and what should you leave out?

Customer and price data

Move customers, contact persons, billing addresses, delivery addresses and established prices. You need these every day for quotes, orders and invoices. Once a customer's details have been entered, they do not need to be retyped for every order.

Transports and documents

Move transports from the moment you start using the program: vehicles, drivers, upcoming runs and the orders for them. Old, paid transports do not need to be moved, because from now on new jobs will run through the program from order to invoice. In RAHTIS, you can also create an order by pasting the customer's email or uploading the order as a PDF or image: the form is filled in for you, and you check it.

What stays in Excel or the archive

Leave out one-off experiments, old quote calculations and reports from closed financial years. Archive the Excel file as it is and do not aim for a complete transfer of history: much of the transfer work would go on data that nobody opens any more. If the Excel file is part of the accounting records, for example a sales ledger or a list of invoices, it must not be destroyed: under the Finnish Accounting Act, vouchers and correspondence relating to business transactions must be kept for at least six years from the end of the year in which the financial year ended, and the books of account and financial statements for ten years (Accounting Act 1336/1997, Chapter 2, section 10, Finlex).

How do you make the switch step by step without disruption?

Step 1: Transfer and clean up the data

From Excel, compile customers, vehicles with their registration numbers, drivers and open receivables into separate lists. Remove duplicates and write customer names consistently, because the program does not know that "Rautainen Oy" and "Rautainen" are the same customer to be invoiced. Check business IDs (Y-tunnus, the unique identifier of a Finnish company) at the same time. Bring over only open transports.

Step 2: A trial run with one vehicle

For two weeks, run all jobs for one vehicle and one driver in the new system: order, run, proof of delivery and invoice. That way any errors affect one vehicle, not the whole business.

Step 3: Training for the office and the drivers

Train dispatch and invoicing first, then the drivers: how to accept a task, how to confirm arrival, how to take photos and how the consignee signs or the consignment note is scanned. In a team that speaks several languages, walk people through the steps on the phone in person rather than relying on written instructions.

Step 4: Parallel running

Parallel running means entering the same transports both in Excel and in the new program until the new program has produced two full billing periods with no missing proofs of delivery or forgotten receivables. At the end of each period, compare the invoice totals line by line. Any discrepancies are usually caused by transfer errors, and they are corrected before Excel is given up.

The same trial principle applies to freight exchanges: RAHTIS has its own load board, where shippers post transports, but it is worth checking the features and prices of TIMOCOM, Teleroute and Trans.eu on their own websites before committing to any service. Excel is closed only once the parallel periods match.

How long should parallel running last, and when can you give up Excel?

Run Excel and the new program in parallel for at least two full billing periods. If you invoice twice a month, that means about a month; if you invoice once a month, about two months. During this time, you enter every load in both and compare the totals and load lists at the end of each period. Once two periods have gone through without significant discrepancies, Excel can be archived.

After that, keep the old spreadsheet read-only as an archive, so that old prices and agreements can be found if a dispute arises later, and so that the Accounting Act's retention periods are met.

So the moment to switch is decided by a measure, not the calendar: no discrepancies in two periods. In RAHTIS, the job, consignment note, proof of delivery and invoice are in the same chain, so the parallel paperwork quickly becomes unnecessary.

Which mistakes derail the switch in a small haulage firm?

The most common mistake is moving everything at once. When Excel, the paper notebook and the old ways all go on the same day, a single problem can bring the whole business to a halt, because the same person handles dispatch, invoicing and everything else. Parallel running keeps invoicing going even if something goes wrong.

The second fatal mistake is an incomplete data transfer. If open transports, proofs of delivery and receivables lists are left in Excel, parallel running drags on and invoicing breaks off in the middle of the month. Move open transports and unpaid invoices; history stays in the archive.

The third mistake is skipping training: drivers and the office need practice, not an email. The fourth is too short a parallel run, under two weeks, when errors do not have time to surface. An honest limitation: if you have years of history in freight exchange accounts or an old program, it will not move into RAHTIS by itself.

What does managing transport in one program mean in practice?

One program replaces the paper notebook, Excel and separate invoicing: orders, runs, proofs of delivery and invoices are in the same chain. In practice, that means three things.

Dispatch and the order chain

A transport is entered once, the vehicle and driver are assigned to it, and the status is visible at a glance. Dispatch information is in the system, not just in your head.

Invoicing and proof of delivery

When the run is completed, the proof of delivery, photos and consignment note are on the same job, and the invoice status is visible in the same service. The EU eFTI Regulation (EU) 2020/1056 requires authorities to accept electronic freight transport information made available via certified eFTI platforms from 9 July 2027; it does not oblige businesses to give up paper (EUR-Lex, eFTI Regulation 2020/1056). It is still worth dismantling the paper chain now, because an electronic chain speeds up invoicing. In RAHTIS, the first vehicle is free, and each additional vehicle that has run jobs during the month costs €14.90 a month (excl. VAT); the first month is free, so a trial costs little.

Load board and backloads

On the RAHTIS load board you can see backloads near your vehicle's delivery point in the same service where your other runs are, so an empty return does not go unnoticed.

How do you get drivers who speak different languages and the office onto the same system?

Language is set per user. The RAHTIS driver app works in ten languages, including Finnish, Swedish, English, Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Polish and Russian, and picks the language from the phone's settings; the driver can change it in their profile. The driver sees tasks, addresses and times in their own language, while the office reads the same transport in Finnish.

During the switch, it is worth training drivers in small groups by language: a short induction on the phone is enough, provided each person does one practice job from start to finish.

Job messages between the shipper, the haulier and the driver are machine-translated, and the original message can be opened alongside. Machine translation is not flawless, so important instructions should be double-checked.

FAQ

Can I switch to the program in the middle of the month, or should I wait for the start of the month?

You can start at any time, but do not change invoicing in the middle of a period. Start the trial and parallel running when you have the fewest open transports, and let the new program produce full billing periods from start to finish. Comparison only works when both systems cover the same period.

Do I need to move my entire Excel history into RAHTIS, or are open transports enough?

It is enough to move customers, vehicles, drivers, open transports and unpaid invoices. Paid and completed transports stay in the archive. Material forming part of the accounting records is kept for the periods set by the Accounting Act, at least six years.

Does one program work when I run my own loads and take loads from a freight exchange?

Yes. Jobs for your own customers, jobs from the RAHTIS load board and transports agreed elsewhere are entered in the same chain: transport, driver, proof of delivery and invoice. RAHTIS does not fetch loads from other exchanges, such as TIMOCOM, Teleroute or Trans.eu, so you enter their details on the job yourself.

How quickly do drivers learn to confirm delivery on their phone?

Usually during a single practice job: show them in person how to accept a task, confirm arrival, take photos and have the consignee sign or scan the consignment note. The app also works offline, and entries are sent when the connection returns.

What do I do if a customer still wants transports as an Excel list?

Run a report from the system and send it to the customer in the format they are used to: RAHTIS reports can be exported as PDF, Excel and CSV. The customer's requirement concerns what they receive, not your tool. Keep the old Excel file as an archive, not as a parallel set of records.
