# Driver pay in PL / EE / LV / LT (+ SE / NO / DK briefly): research for RAHTIS pay templates

Status: research note, 5.11.2026. Not legal advice. Every number has a source link next to it.
**Verify** = I found the number only in secondary sources or a draft, or I couldn't confirm the 2026 value.
Like the Finnish TES template, every template below is a starting point that the carrier edits. The calculator stays *indicative* (PL "orientacyjny", EE "soovituslik", LV "indikatīvs", LT "orientacinis").

## 0. EU layer (applies to all EU countries below)

| Rule | What it means for pay | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Posting of drivers** (Dir. (EU) 2020/1057 art. 1(3)–(7)) | **Not posted:** bilateral trips to/from the country of establishment, plus up to 1 (2) extra loading/unloading on the way, and transit. **Posted:** cabotage and cross-trade (e.g. a PL truck driving DE→FR). While posted, the driver must get at least the host country's **remuneration** (minimum wage and allowances made universally applicable) for those days/hours. | [EUR-Lex 32020L1057](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2020/1057/oj) |
| **What counts toward the host minimum** (Dir. 96/71 art. 3(7) as amended by 2018/957) | Allowances specific to posting count as remuneration **unless** they reimburse expenses actually incurred (travel, board, lodging). If the contract doesn't say which part is reimbursement, the **whole allowance is treated as reimbursement**, so none of it counts. This means per diems normally **do not** top up to the host minimum wage. | [EUR-Lex 32018L0957](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2018/957/oj) |
| **Rest in the cab** (Reg. 2020/1054 amending 561/2006 art. 8(8), 8(8a)) | Reduced weekly rests and daily rests may be taken in the cab. **Regular weekly rest (≥45 h) may not**: the employer must pay for suitable accommodation. The driver must be able to return home every 4 weeks (3 with two reduced rests). For pay this means hotel costs for regular weekly rest abroad are an **employer cost**, not something taken from per diems. | [EUR-Lex 32020R1054](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2020/1054/oj) |
| **Pay tied to km/tonnes** (Reg. 561/2006 art. 10(1); PL also art. 26 ust. 1 ustawy o czasie pracy kierowców) | Paying drivers by distance or amount of goods is **forbidden if it endangers road safety or encourages rule breaking**. Bonuses per km aren't banned outright, but pay that is mostly per km is a compliance risk in every EU country. | [EUR-Lex 32006R0561](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/561/oj), [Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1473](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2022/1473/text.html) |
| Example host minimum: Germany | 13.90 €/h from 1.1.2026, 14.60 €/h from 1.1.2027 | [Bloomberg Tax – DE gazette](https://news.bloombergtax.com/daily-tax-report/germany-gazettes-regulation-increasing-2026-2027-hourly-minimum-wage-rates) |

Calculator consequence: for each day we need the **activity type** (domestic / bilateral / transit / cross-trade / cabotage) and the **country**. On posted days the gross *wage* part (per diems excluded) must be ≥ host minimum × hours. Below that, the calculator shows a "posting top-up missing" warning.

### 1.2 Per diems: two separate regimes since 2022
**(a) Domestic transport.** The driver is on a *podróż służbowa* (UCPK art. 2 pkt 7, art. 21a) and gets allowances under the Labour Code regulation ([Rozp. MPiPS 29.1.2013, Dz.U. 2013 poz. 167](https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/DocDetails.xsp?id=WDU20130000167)):
- **Dieta krajowa 45 PLN/day** (since 1.1.2023). A draft raising it to **60 PLN** (ryczałt za nocleg 90 PLN, hotel cap 1 200 PLN) was still a draft in Oct 2026, now aimed at 1.1.2027. **Verify** before 2027. Sources: [pit.pl 2026 stawki](https://www.pit.pl/aktualnosci/podroze-sluzbowe-i-diety-pracownicze-stawki-obowiazujace-w-2026-roku-1011911), [forsal – projekt 21.5.2026](https://forsal.pl/praca/aktualnosci/artykuly/11338503,czy-beda-wyzsze-diety-krajowe-i-zagraniczne-od-1-stycznia-2027-r-co-z-noclegami-podczas-delegacji-projekt-rozporzadzenia.html), [infor – draft details](https://kadry.infor.pl/kadry/wynagrodzenia/podroze_sluzbowe/7506987,podwyzki-w-podrozach-sluzbowych-za-hotel-do-1200-zl-2-dni-podrozy-120-zl-a-nie-90-zl-diety-do-gory-ryczalty.html)
- **Partial days:** trip ≤ 24 h: <8 h → 0, 8–12 h → 50 %, >12 h → 100 %. Trip > 24 h: each full 24 h → 100 %; remainder ≤ 8 h → 50 %, > 8 h → 100 %. Free meals reduce the dieta: breakfast −25 %, lunch −50 %, dinner −25 % (§ 7 of the regulation).
- **Tax:** PIT-free and ZUS-free up to these amounts (PIT art. 21 ust. 1 pkt 16 lit. a). Per diems are **not wage**, so they do **not** count toward the minimum wage.

**(b) International transport.** Since 2.2022 a driver in international road transport is **not on a podróż służbowa** (UCPK art. 21b ust. 1, [Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1473](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2022/1473/text.html)). There is no statutory dieta. What employers still call "diety" / "ryczałt zagraniczny" / "dniówka" is legally **remuneration**: it is taxed and counts toward the minimum wage. Two reliefs apply instead, introduced by the act on posting drivers ([Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1523](https://www.infor.pl/akt-prawny/DZU.2023.216.0001523,ustawa-o-delegowaniu-kierowcow-w-transporcie-drogowym.html)):
- **PIT:** income exempt up to **20 EUR per day abroad** (PIT art. 21 ust. 1 pkt 23d). Days abroad < 12 h count as ⅓ (≤ 8 h) or ½ (8–12 h). Leave and sick days don't count.
- **ZUS:** the contribution base is reduced by **60 EUR per day abroad** (the "wirtualna dieta"). This applies only if monthly income exceeds the average wage, and the base may not fall **below the average wage (9 420 PLN in 2026)**.
- Also exempt or excluded: reimbursed **documented** costs (lodging, travel to the truck by other than a company vehicle, sanitary facilities).
- Sources: [poradnikprzedsiebiorcy – rozliczanie kierowców międzynarodowych](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-rozliczanie-kierowcow-miedzynarodowych), [trans.info](https://trans.info/pl/ustawa-o-delegowaniu-kierowcow-w-transporcie-drogowym-ofni-355493). A Sejm print proposed raising 60 → 90 EUR; **not confirmed as enacted** ([tachospeed](https://tachospeed.pl/?p=116914)).

**Foreign dieta rates** (needed for domestic-employer business trips by non-drivers, and widely used as the contract "reference" for driver allowances), from the annex to the 2013 regulation. Germany 49 EUR, France 50 EUR, Czechia 41 EUR, Italy/Spain 48 EUR ([infor table 2026](https://kadry.infor.pl/kadry/wynagrodzenia/podroze_sluzbowe/6583104,dieta-zagraniczna-delegacja-2026-niemcy-usa-litwa-austria-szwecja-czechy-i-inne-panstwa-tabela.html)). The pending draft changes many of them (e.g. CZ 41→45, SE/NO/DK switched to EUR). **Verify** when it's published.

### 1.3 Accommodation lump sums (ryczałt za nocleg)
- Domestic: **150 % of dieta = 67.50 PLN** per night without a hotel invoice. Not due when free lodging is provided. Foreign (business trips): **25 % of the country's hotel limit** (e.g. DE limit 150/170 EUR depending on source → 37.50–42.50 EUR; draft raises DE to 220 EUR). Sources: [Rozp. 2013 § 8, § 16](https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.nsf/DocDetails.xsp?id=WDU20130000167), [infakt list](https://www.infakt.pl/blog/?p=49543)
- **Cab sleeping:** a long dispute (SN uchwała 7 sędziów II PZP 1/14 of 12.6.2014 – cab ≠ free accommodation; TK K 11/15 of 2016; 2017 amendment saying no lump sum if the cab has a suitable sleeping place). Since 2022 international drivers have no ryczałt at all (not a business trip). **Recommendation:** for domestic trips, the template defaults to "no ryczałt for nights in a cab with a sleeping place" with a toggle. Mark it indicative.
- Regular weekly rest (≥45 h): employer must pay for accommodation (EU 2020/1054). This is a cost line, not driver pay.

**Per diems:** the long-haul driver is sent on a *välislähetus* (TLS § 21). Tax-free **75 €/day for the first 15 days of a trip, max 15 such days per calendar month, then 40 €/day** (TuMS § 13 lg 3 p 1, since 1.1.2025). Statutory **minimum 40 €** (VV määrus nr 110). The employer may cut it by up to 70 % if free meals are provided. Anything above the limit is taxed as wage. Departure day counts if the vehicle leaves by 21:00. Domestic trips: no tax-free per diem (only documented costs). Sources: [EMTA välislähetuse päevaraha](https://emta.ee/ariklient/maksud-ja-tasumine/tulumaks-ja-sotsiaalmaks/erisoodustused/valislahetuse-paevaraha), [EMTA kaugsõiduautojuhid](https://emta.ee/ariklient/maksud-ja-tasumine/tulumaks-ja-sotsiaalmaks/erisoodustused/paevaraha-maksmine-kaugsoiduautojuhtidele), [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/ee/et/insights/2024/12/infokuller---muudatused-maksuseadustes.html).
**Accommodation:** reimbursement of documented costs only (no lump sum). Cab nights: nothing.
**Schemes:** EE-A minimum-wage-plus base (hourly/monthly, + night 25 %, overtime 50 %) + päevaraha 75/40 → **legal**. EE-B low base + per diem above 75/40 paid tax-free → **illegal** above the limit. EE-C per-km/% → bonus only.
**Calculator detail:** "15 days at 75 € per *trip*, capped at 15 per calendar month" needs a trip ID and the trip start date. See the EMTA examples.

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum wage | **780 €/month from 1.1.2026** (2025: 740). Hourly = 780 / normal hours in the month (MK 656 formula). Planned 820 € (2027), 860 € (2028). | [LM.gov.lv](https://www.lm.gov.lv/en/article/minimum-wage-latvia-will-be-780-euros), [likumi.lv MK 656](https://m.likumi.lv/ta/id/278067) |
| Night work (22–06, >2 h) | Supplement ≥ **50 %** (Darba likums 67. p.) | [lvportals 2026](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38920-darba-samaksa-ja-svetku-diena-jastrada-nakts-darbs-2026) |
| Overtime / public holiday | ≥ **100 %** (DL 68. p.). Not adopted as of the last check (April 2026). **Verify.** | [lvportals 387253](https://lvportals.lv/skaidrojumi/387253-virsstundu-un-darba-svetku-diena-apmaksas-regulejuma-izmainas-raisa-plasu-rezonansi-2026), [LSM 14.4.2026](https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/zinas/ekonomika/14.04.2026-par-stridigajam-darba-likuma-izmainam-varetu-nakties-lemt-nakamajam-saeimas-sasaukumam.a642892/) |
| Working time | Drivers often on summed working time (DL 140. p.), averaged over the accounting period. | [lvportals 38341](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38341-darba-un-atputas-laika-rezims-summeta-darba-laika-darbiniekam-2026) |
| Sector agreement | None found for road freight. | — |

**Per diems (MK noteikumi Nr. 969, 2010):** a truck driver is on a **darba brauciens**, i.e. work on the road (p. 3).
- Per day, the driver gets "additional expenses" at the per diem rate of the country (Annex 1; e.g. **Germany 50 €**, France 60 € — **verify** the current annex) (p. 42).
- **Cap:** monthly total ≤ **70 % of (calculated wage + calculated additional expenses)** (p. 42), i.e. per diems ≤ 2.33 × wage.
- Not due if the driver returns home within the same 24 h and rest rules are respected (p. 43). Reduction to ≤ 30 % only if free accommodation + 3 meals are provided (p. 13).
- Posted workers (DL 14.² (5)): 30 % of the rate.
- Within the norms: free of IIN and VSAOI.
- Sources: [lvportals 37557 (VDI)](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/37557-komandejuma-un-darba-brauciena-dienas-naudas-izmaksa-2025), [lvportals 38341](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/38341-darba-un-atputas-laika-rezims-summeta-darba-laika-darbiniekam-2026), [MK 969 on vestnesis](https://www.vestnesis.lv/ta/id/220013-kartiba-kada-atlidzinami-ar-komandejumiem-saistitie-izdevumi), [DE rate](https://lvportals.lv/e-konsultacijas/21251-komandejuma-dienas-nauda-vacija-2020).

**Accommodation:** documented costs up to the annex hotel limit (e.g. DE 180 €, big cities 220 €). No lump sum for the cab.
**Schemes:** LV-A minimum-wage base + dienas nauda within the 70 % cap → **legal** (this is the mainstream model; the cap makes "low base + big per diem" lawful as long as the base ≥ 780 € and the rates ≤ annex). LV-B "envelope" or per diem above the annex rate / above the cap → **illegal**. LV-C per-km → bonus only.

**Per diems (dienpinigiai):** mandatory abroad (DK 107 str.). Ceilings per country come from Government resolution No. 526 (2004) and its annex (**verify** the current rates on e-tar.lt). The contract may set lower amounts. Allowed reductions: one-shift trip and border-crossing day up to 50 %, free meals ([trans.info LT](https://trans.info/lt/vilkiku-vairuotojams-mokami-dienpinigiai-viskas-ka-apie-tai-turi-zinoti-darbdavys-ir-darbuotojas-79995)).
**Tax-free conditions (GPMĮ 17 str. 1 d. 5 p., Resolution No. 99 Taisyklės):**
- Full per diem is GPM/Sodra-free **if the driver's set wage ≥ 1.65 × MMA** (2025: 1 712.70 €; **2026 = 1 902.45 € or hourly 11.63 €**, if the coefficient is unchanged; **verify**).
- Otherwise only per diems ≤ **50 % of the set wage** per month are tax-free. The excess is taxed as wage (20 % GPM + Sodra).
- Source: [VMI 2025 presentation](https://gamtostyrimai.lt/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/9-Del-MMA-ir-dienpinigiu-apmokestinimo_2025_VMI.pdf).

Per diems are compensation, not wage. They don't count toward MMA.
**Accommodation:** documented costs within the resolution limits. No cab lump sum.
**Schemes:** LT-A base ≥ 1.65 MMA + full dienpinigiai → **legal, tax-optimal** (the market standard). LT-B base = MMA + dienpinigiai (taxed above 50 % of wage) → **legal** but costlier. LT-C base below MMA "topped up" with dienpinigiai → **illegal**. Market take-home 1 800–3 000 €/month ([cvbankas ads](https://www.cvbankas.lt/tolimuju-reisu-vairuotojas-2-savaites-dirbi-1-laisva-labai-patogus-darbo-grafikas-lietuviskas-kolektyvas-vilniuje/1-4291233); ads, not statistics).

## 6. What a calculator needs

### Inputs (per driver per day, mostly derivable from existing `driver_shifts` + orders)
| Input | Why | Where from in RAHTIS |
|---|---|---|
| Shift start/end, breaks | hours, night hours, overtime, partial-day per diem | `driver_shifts`, `driver_breaks` (exists) |
| **Country segments per day** (country, from/to time) | per diem rate per country, PL ⅓/½ partial days, EE 21:00 rule, posting | NEW: derive from GPS/telematics border crossings or order stops; manual override |
| **Activity type per segment**: DOMESTIC / BILATERAL / TRANSIT / CROSS_TRADE / CABOTAGE | posting → host minimum; PL domestic vs international regime | NEW: from order loading/unloading countries vs company country; manual override |
| **Trip id + trip start** (away-from-home trip) | EE 15-day counter; LT one-shift trip; PL domestic >24 h rule | NEW: `driver_trips` (group of days away) or derive from home-base departure/return |
| Nights: HOTEL (invoice) / CAB / FREE / HOME | ryczałt za nocleg, accommodation costs | NEW field per night |
| Meals provided (B/L/D) | PL reductions, EE/LV/LT cuts | optional per day |
| km (odometer / trips) | per-km bonus | exists (`payableKm`) |
| Freight payout | % of freight bonus | exists (TRIP_PERCENT) |
| Public holidays per country | holiday supplements | extend `finnishHolidays()` to a `holidays(country, year)` |
| FX rate per day | EUR per diems paid in PLN (PL uses the NBP rate of the day before payment) | `fx_rates` (exists) |

### Outputs
1. **Gross wage** split: base, night, overtime, holiday, bonuses (km / %), **posting top-up** (if host minimum isn't met).
2. **Per diems / allowances** separately: tax-free part vs taxable part (PL 20 EUR PIT / 60 EUR ZUS reliefs; EE 75/40 cap; LT 50 % / 1.65 MMA test; LV 70 % cap).
3. **Accommodation**: lump sums (PL domestic) and employer-paid hotel costs.
4. **Checks / warnings**: wage < national minimum; posted days below host minimum; per diem above legal ceiling; per-km share dominant; regular weekly rest in cab.
5. **Employer cost (simple, optional)**: gross × employer-contribution % + per diems + accommodation. Rates are template parameters, indicative only (PL ~20.48 % + ZUS base floor; EE 33 % social tax + 0.8 % unemployment; LV 23.59 %; LT 1.77 %). Labelled "orientacyjnie / indicative", **verify** rates.

## 7. Proposed data model extension

Keep `pay_model` and `tes_rule_sets` as they are. Add a country-aware **pay template** layer that the TES engine can reuse.

```sql
-- 1) Generalise rule sets: a TES rule set becomes one kind of "wage rule set"
alter table public.tes_rule_sets
  add column country char(2) not null default 'FI',          -- FI, PL, EE, LV, LT, SE, NO, DK
  add column template_code text,                            -- 'FI_TES_KUORMA', 'PL_INTL_BASE_ALLOWANCE', ...
  add column min_monthly_cents integer,                     -- national minimum for the check
  add column min_hourly_cents integer,
  add column night_basis text not null default 'OWN_RATE'   -- OWN_RATE | MIN_WAGE_HOURLY (PL art.151⁸)
    check (night_basis in ('OWN_RATE','MIN_WAGE_HOURLY')),
  add column holiday_bps integer not null default 0,        -- EE/LV/LT holiday supplements
  add column overtime_night_bps integer,                    -- LT 2×, PL 100 %
  add column indicative boolean not null default true,      -- always shown with disclaimer
  add column legality text not null default 'LEGAL'         -- LEGAL | GREY | NON_COMPLIANT (warning level)
    check (legality in ('LEGAL','GREY','NON_COMPLIANT')),
  add column source_note text;                              -- citations shown in UI, like the TES note

-- 2) Per diem / allowance rule sets (separate from wage rules; a profile can use both)
create table public.allowance_rule_sets (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  company_id uuid references public.companies(id) on delete cascade,  -- null = operator template
  country char(2) not null,                -- employer country
  template_code text not null,             -- 'PL_DOMESTIC_DIETA','PL_INTL_RELIEF','EE_VALISLAHETUS','LV_DARBA_BRAUCIENS','LT_DIENPINIGIAI','SE_TRAKTAMENTE','DK_KOST'
  valid_from date not null,
  currency public.currency_code not null,
  regime text not null check (regime in ('STATUTORY_PER_DIEM','CONTRACT_ALLOWANCE_AS_WAGE')),
  partial_day_rule text not null,          -- 'PL_DOMESTIC' | 'PL_FOREIGN_THIRDS' | 'EE_DEPART_2100' | 'SE_NOON_19' | 'FULL_DAY_ONLY'
  cap_rule text,                           -- 'EE_15_DAYS_PER_TRIP_MONTH' | 'LV_70PCT_OF_WAGE_PLUS' | 'LT_50PCT_OR_165MMA' | 'PL_PIT20_ZUS60' | null
  cap_params jsonb not null default '{}',  -- {"first_days":15,"first_cents":7500,"later_cents":4000} / {"pit_eur_cents":2000,"zus_eur_cents":6000,"zus_floor_cents":942000} / {"coef_bps":16500,"mma_cents":115300}
  meal_reduction_bps jsonb not null default '{}', -- {"B":2500,"L":5000,"D":2500}
  lodging_lump_bps integer,                -- PL: 15000 of domestic dieta; 2500 of foreign hotel limit
  cab_night_lump boolean not null default false,
  posted_rate_bps integer,                 -- LV posted: 3000
  indicative boolean not null default true,
  legality text not null default 'LEGAL',
  source_note text,
  based_on uuid references public.allowance_rule_sets(id) on delete set null
);

create table public.allowance_rates (      -- per destination country (annex tables), editable copy per company
  rule_set_id uuid not null references public.allowance_rule_sets(id) on delete cascade,
  dest_country char(2) not null,           -- 'DE','FR', ... ; employer country = domestic rate
  currency public.currency_code not null,  -- PL foreign diets are in EUR even if payroll is PLN
  day_cents integer not null,
  hotel_limit_cents integer,
  primary key (rule_set_id, dest_country)
);

-- 3) Host-country posting minimums (for cabotage / cross-trade checks)
create table public.posting_minimums (
  country char(2) not null,
  valid_from date not null,
  currency public.currency_code not null,
  hourly_cents integer not null,           -- DE 1390 from 2026-01-01
  note text,
  primary key (country, valid_from)
);

-- 4) Day facts the engine needs
create table public.driver_day_segments (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  driver_id uuid not null references public.drivers(id) on delete cascade,
  trip_key uuid,                           -- groups days of one away-from-home trip
  started_at timestamptz not null,
  ended_at timestamptz not null,
  country char(2) not null,
  activity text not null check (activity in ('DOMESTIC','BILATERAL','TRANSIT','CROSS_TRADE','CABOTAGE')),
  source text not null default 'MANUAL',   -- MANUAL | GPS | ORDER
  night_type text check (night_type in ('HOTEL','CAB','FREE','HOME')),
  meals text                               -- e.g. 'BLD'
);

-- 5) Profile links both parts
alter table public.driver_pay_profiles
  add column allowance_rule_set_id uuid references public.allowance_rule_sets(id) on delete restrict,
  add column bonus_per_km_cents integer,   -- km as BONUS on top of a base (vs PER_KM model as the whole pay)
  add column bonus_trip_bps integer;
-- new pay_model value: 'BASE_MONTHLY' (monthly salary ≥ minimum, prorated) next to FLAT_HOURLY / TES
```

**Engine split (driverPay.ts):** `computeWage(day, ruleSet)` (existing TES logic generalised: base, night with `night_basis`, overtime DAY/PERIOD, holidays via `holidays(country)`), `computeAllowances(segments, allowanceSet)` (partial days, meals, caps that need month context → run in `summarizeDriver`), `checks(summary)` (national minimum, posting minimum per posted hour, caps, per-km share > X %). Everything returns `indicative: true` + `legality`.
