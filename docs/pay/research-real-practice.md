# How drivers are really paid (PL / LT / LV / EE + FI / SE / NO / DK / DE): agreed pay, paper treatment, risk

Status: research note, 6.11.2026. Not legal or tax advice. This file sits next to `research-driver-pay-countries.md` (legal framework) and `seed-data.json` (verified 2026 numbers), and does not repeat them.

**Scope.** RAHTIS models the pay a driver and carrier *agreed* (what the driver is owed under the agreement). Next to it, it shows the **legal payroll treatment** of the same days and the **risk** if the paper version differs. RAHTIS does not model how to hide income and does not track cash paid off the books.

**Labels:**
- **[V] verified:** law, official body, court ruling, or press reporting an official act or statistic.
- **[M] market practice:** job ads, recruiter guides, surveys, union or driver statements, forums. Ads are not statistics.
- **[U] unverified / gap:** a lead only, not for seeding.

---

## 0. Cross-country picture

1. **The day rate is the main unit of agreement** in PL, LT, LV and EE. It is quoted as net "in hand": *dniówka netto*, *€/diena į rankas*, *€ dienā*, *€ päevas*, *€ в день на руки*. It is paid per day on the road, sometimes per day of the whole rotation. Monthly net offers ("3 000 € net") are the second most common form. Per-km pay is rare in the Baltics and is a top-up in PL (§1). Percentage of freight appears mostly for owner-drivers and subcontractors. [M; sources per country]
2. **The usual paper form** is a base near the national minimum (LT: often 1.65 × MMA, the per diem tax threshold), plus per diems / "dodatek zagraniczny" that make up the agreed net. Shares reported:
   - EE union: "a large share" of long-haul pay is per diem [M, ERR 2026].
   - Belarusian drivers in PL/LT: "the official salary is often about half the total" [M, nashaniva.com/ru/388123].
   - LT Sodra: long-haul drivers' average insured income is ~1 750 €, and almost 1/5 are insured at MMA or below [V, Sodra via trans.info 478416].
3. **Where per diems stay within legal norms, this structure is lawful** (LV 70 % cap; LT ≤ Res. 526 norms with base ≥ 1.65 MMA; EE 75/40; PL 20 € PIT / 60 € ZUS reliefs). **The grey part** is any per diem above the norm or cap that is treated as tax-free, a base below the minimum, pay outside payroll, or B2B contracts for drivers who are in reality employees.
4. **Since 2026, enforcement has shifted toward third-country drivers:**
   - LT permits after the quota is used up: wage ≥ 1.2 × average wage (2 893 €).
   - LV permits: ≥ transport-sector average (1 637 € gross).
   - PL fines per foreigner.
   - Host-country back-pay claims in NL/DE, with seizure of the carrier's receivables from clients.
5. **Per diems and the host minimum (posting).** Under Dir. 96/71 art. 3(7) (as amended by 2018/957), allowances count toward the host minimum only if they are not reimbursement of expenses. If the contract does not split them, they are treated as reimbursement and do not count. Some industry summaries (e.g. IRU) say per diems "count as remuneration". The calculator keeps the conservative reading: **per diems do not fill the host minimum unless the contract labels a part as wage.** [V, EUR-Lex 32018L0957; see research-driver-pay-countries.md §0]

### 1.2 How it appears on paper
- **[M]** Infor, citing the TLP "Raport drogowy 2024/2025": pay is often agreed as a fixed net per day apart from the formal rules. 75 % of transport firms encounter "pay outside official channels" (this is a share of firms, not drivers). [infor](https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/obrot-gospodarczy/dzialalnosc-gospodarcza/6879695,praktyka-placenia-pracownikom-pod-stolem-to-ciagly-problem-w-firmach-transportowych-jakie-sa-tego-skutki-dla-przewoznikow-i-calej-branzy.html)
- **[V] Lawful mapping of a dniówka.** The base must be ≥ 4 806 PLN. Then a contractual daily allowance per day abroad, treated as **wage**: PIT-free up to 20 EUR/day, ZUS base reduced by 60 EUR/day, but not below 9 420 PLN (10 033 PLN in the 2027 draft budget). Trans.info's expert: contributions are effectively paid up to about the 18th day abroad, after which extra days are contribution-free. Employer cost example (10 000 PLN net, 20 days abroad): ~14 809 PLN/month in 2026 → ~15 072 PLN in 2027. [trans.info 507213](https://trans.info/pl/wyzszy-zus-dla-kierowcow-miedzynarodowych-od-2027-r-ile-moze-wzrosnac-koszt-przewoznika-507213)
- **Grey forms:**
  - "Minimum + diety" treated as tax-free beyond the 20/60 EUR reliefs. Since 2022 these are wage for international drivers.
  - Fake B2B/JDG.
  - Umowa zlecenie: ZUS treats it like employment with the same floor ([podatki.biz](https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/zus-kierowca-na-umowie-zleceniu-oskladkowany-jak-pracownik_10_51711.htm)).
- **[V] Rates 2026:** employer ZUS 20.48 % (accident insurance 1.67 % default); employee 13.71 %; health 9 %; PIT 12 %/32 %. [poradnikprzedsiebiorcy](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-skladki-zus-za-pracownikow-stopy-procentowe-i-zrodla-ich-finansowania-2026)

### 1.3 Enforcement and risk [V]
- **PIP reform in force 8.7.2026.** PIP can reclassify B2B or civil contracts as employment by administrative decision. It gets data sharing with ZUS and KAS. Fines rise to 2 000–60 000 PLN (90 000 for repeat offenders); inspectors' on-the-spot fines go up to 10 000 PLN. [infor](https://kadry.infor.pl/zatrudnienie/umowa-o-prace/7620175,surowe-kary-dla-pracodawcy-od-8-lipca-2026-r-kontrola-pip-po-nowemu-i-nowe-wykroczenie-obejscie-przepisow-dot-zawierania-umow-cywilnoprawnych.html)
- **B2B drivers.** Reclassification indicators: fixed schedule, the carrier's truck and fuel, mileage pay, a single client. Consequences: ZUS on the full invoiced amounts plus interest, PIT correction, holiday pay in lieu, overtime and night supplements, and risk to "good repute" and so to the operating licence (Reg. 1071/2009). [trans.info 500168](https://trans.info/pl/fikcyjne-b2b-kierowcow-ciezarowek-na-celowniku-pip-sprawdz-czy-kontrakt-nie-stal-sie-etatem-500168)
- **Deductions.** Unlawful deductions are an offence (KP art. 282). Damage needs written consent and is capped at 3 months' pay (art. 119). Disciplinary fines are capped at 1/10 of monthly pay. Minimum-wage pay is protected. Deductions from "diety" need consent (WSA). [poradnikprzedsiebiorcy](https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-potracenie-szkody-w-mieniu-pracodawcy), [rp.pl](https://pro.rp.pl/place/art2399201-potracenie-z-diet-kierowcy-tylko-za-jego-zgoda-wyrok-wsa)
- **Foreigners.** Since 1.6.2025, illegal employment carries 3 000–50 000 PLN, at least 3 000 per foreigner. Radom case: of 131 drivers checked, 58 were working on terms different from their permits; the company president was fined 25 000 PLN on 9.9.2026. [trans.info 504850](https://trans.info/pl/zezwolenie-to-nie-wszystko-58-kierowcow-pracowalo-nielegalnie-prezes-firmy-ukarany-504850)
- **Posting.** The roadside check window was extended to 56 days. ITD lacks access to IMI declarations (ZMPD). [rp.pl](https://pro.rp.pl/prawo-w-firmie/art40934741-nieszczelne-przepisy-o-delegowaniu-kierowcow-o-kierowcy-zawiadomic-trzeba-pip)
- **Legacy.** The cab-night ryczałt litigation (TK K 11/15) ended with SN II PSKP 77/22: no legal basis for the claims. [SN](https://www.sn.pl/sites/orzecznictwo/Orzeczenia3/II%20PSKP%2077-22.pdf)
- **PIP 2026** lists driver working time as an inspection priority. [forsal](https://forsal.pl/artykuly/1427799,pip-pracodawcy-nadal-naruszaja-przepisy-dotyczace-czasu-pracy)
- **Gaps [U]:** the transport chapter of the PIP report, GITD/IMI statistics, and any published ZUS reclassification amounts.

### 2.2 How it appears on paper
- **[V/M]** The base is at MMA (1 153 €) or 1.65 × MMA (1 902.45 €, the per diem tax-free threshold). The agreed day rate is reached by adjusting dienpinigiai up to the Res. 526 norm (DE 62 €, FR 69 €, NL 64 €...).
- **[V]** Sodra: average insured income of long-haul drivers is ~1 750 €.
- **[V/U]** VMI protocol of 25.5.2026 (snippet only): after mobile-work compensation was abolished in 2023, per diems in some transport firms rose up to 3×. [vmi.lt PDF](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/documents/20142/1097704/Protokolas+2026-05-25.pdf/57a11938-cdf6-6fab-974d-7a683201a14f?t=1780478182307)
- **[V] Res. 518 (in force 1.10.2026) narrows "variable per diem" pay:**
  - Cuts are allowed by internal act only for trips of 7+ days, and only down to ≥ 65 % of the norm. For trips under 7 days, cuts need a collective agreement, with a floor of 50 %.
  - The rate must be given in writing before departure and is fixed for the trip.
  - A first or last day under 4 h pays 20 %.
  - Lawyers warn that cutting per diems for damage, discipline or as a reward is risky.
  - Sources: [Linava](https://www.linava.lt/naujienos/pakeistas-vyriausybes-nutarimas-del-dienpinigiu-mokejimo/), [teise.pro](https://www.teise.pro/index.php/2026/07/16/dienpinigiu-mazinimas-nuo-spalio-1-d-aiskesnes-taisykles/), [trans.info 500408](https://trans.info/lt/vezejams-keiciasi-dienpinigiu-mokejimo-tvarka-500408)
- **[V] Third-country drivers.**
  - 2026 quota is 24 706, of which 22 552 were used by 21.8.
  - From 1.9.2026, post-quota permits need a wage of **≥ 1.2 × average wage = 2 893 €**, paid for the whole permit period (1 × average wage = 2 411 € for shortage-list jobs; whether CE drivers are on the list is [U]).
  - This makes "MMA + per diems" unusable for new non-EU hires after the quota runs out.
  - Source: [vezejas.eu](https://vezejas.eu/migracijos-departamentas-informuoja-keiciasi-tarpininkavimo-rastu-ir-prasymu-kuriems-taikoma-uzsienieciu-idarbinimo-kvota-teikimo-proceduros/)
- **[V] Rates 2026:** employee Sodra 19.5 % (22.5 % with 2nd pillar), GPM 20 %. Employer guarantee fund 0.16 % and long-term employment fund 0.16 %. Employer Sodra 1.77 % is **[U]** (secondary source; fixed-term contracts are higher). [mokesciu-sufleris](https://www.mokesciu-sufleris.lt/product/download/sodros-imoku-atmintine-2026-m.html), [KPMG LT](https://kpmg.com/lt/lt/izvalgos/mokesciu-ir-teises-naujienlaiskiai/2026-sausis.html)

### 2.3 Enforcement and risk
- **[V] Labour dispute commissions:** 1 700+ claims in 2025 with 1.8 M € awarded. In Q1 2026, 0.5 M €+ awarded, with ~65 % of claimants foreigners (UZ/TJ/BY). Prosecutors are investigating 6+ logistics firms. 158 companies have lost the right to invite foreigners. [trans.info 478416](https://trans.info/lt/migrantu-vairuotojai-lietuvoje-viliojami-atlyginimais-dalis-lieka-su-skolomis-478416)
- **[V] Migration ban.** A 6-month ban on inviting or extending foreign workers can follow even a minor reporting fine. An appeal does not suspend it. [trans.info 457199](https://trans.info/lt/migracijos-departamento-sankcijos-transporto-bendrovems-457199)
- **[V] Court cases:**
  - Klaipėda District Court (published 13.11.2025): per diems withheld at contract end and paid below the norms → 4 400 € per diems + 10 600 € penalties. [trans.info 442854](https://trans.info/en/foreign-driver-allowances-442854)
  - Overijssel (NL, 3/2026): a Tajik driver could keep the truck as security; ~18 000 € awarded. [trans.info 462455](https://trans.info/en/court-orders-wage-payment-462455)
  - Cargo24 (NL, 6/2026): four African drivers claim 232 000 € under Dutch pay. The court allowed attachment of the carrier's receivables from clients (151 000 € on deposit). FNV is pressing the shippers; DFDS ended its contract. [trans.info 479892](https://trans.info/lt/sunkvezimiu-vairuotojai-pries-lietuvos-vezeja-232-tukst-eur-gincas-del-atlyginimu-479892), [trans.info 483763](https://trans.info/en/african-drivers-wage-claim-483763)
  - LPS "Solidarumas" case: lump sums labelled "wage + per diems" cannot later be clawed back as per diem "overpayment" (DK art. 150 limits set-off). [lps.lt](https://www.lps.lt/vairuotojas-laimejo-byla-pries-darbdavi-sumoketa-permoka-yra-darbo-uzmokestis/)
- **Tax reclassification.** Excess per diems become wage: GPM + Sodra + late-payment interest + a fine of 10–50 % of the additional tax (MAĮ art. 139). The fine range is **[U]**, not re-read in primary text. No published 2023–26 VMI per diem case with amounts was found.

### 3.2 How it appears on paper
- **[V]** Lawful model: base ≥ 780 € plus dienas nauda at MK 969 rates, with the monthly total ≤ 70 % of (wage + allowances). Within the norms, allowances are free of IIN and VSAOI. Above the norms, they are wage ([irgrisinajumi](https://irgrisinajumi.lv/komandejuma-dienas-nauda/)). In practice the agreed day rate is split between wage and allowance; LSM notes ads "slightly above minimum on paper".
- **[V] Third-country permits** require pay ≥ the transport-sector average (1 637 € gross for 2024). Uzbekistan supplies nearly 3 000 drivers; TJ, IN, UA and BY are also top sources ([LSM 16.9.2025](https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/zinas/ekonomika/16.09.2025-lielakas-algas-viesstradnieki-pasbraucosi-auto-ka-risinat-valsti-samilzuso-soferu-trukumu.a614411/)). Whether the threshold means base wage only is [U].
- **[V] State Audit Office (9/2026):** ~40 % of all work permits go to truck drivers. In 18 % of audited cases no employment was registered after the permit. Third-country workers posted into LV from other EU states rose from 972 to 1 863, costing ~5 M € of labour taxes. [LSM 9.9.2026](https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/zinas/ekonomika/09.09.2026-valsts-kontrole-arvalstu-darbaspeka-piesaistei-trukst-skaidras-politikas-darba-atlauju-uzraudziba-ir-caurumi.a662316/)
- **[V] Rates 2026:** VSAOI employer 23.59 %, employee 10.5 %; IIN 25.5 %; non-taxable minimum 550 €/month. [fm.gov.lv](https://www.fm.gov.lv/lv/media/25304/download)

### 3.3 Enforcement and risk [V]
- **VID labour tax gap (2022 data).** "Land and pipeline transport" ranks #2 by undeclared pay (169.4 M €). VID flags workplaces whose hourly rate is far below the regional norm for the job, and compares job ads. [lvportals 354867](https://lvportals.lv/norises/354867-darbaspeka-nodoklu-plaisa-mazinas-bet-aploksnu-algu-tradicija-nemainas-2023)
- **Criminal case** (LSM 7.8.2024): a freight company paid ~1.2 M € off the books in 2019–23, evading ~1 M € of taxes. Property was arrested. Charges under Criminal Law §217.1 and §218(2). [LSM](https://www.lsm.lv/raksts/zinas/ekonomika/07.08.2024-ludz-sakt-kriminalvajasanu-par-12-miljoniem-eiro-aploksnu-algas-kravu-parvadajumu-uznemuma.a564222/)
- **Interest** 0.05 %/day (PwC: capped at 40 % of principal), plus separate fines. A settlement waives 85 % / 75 % of the interest. [PwC LV](https://www.pwc.com/lv/lv/news/nokavejuma-naudas-labirinti.html), [KPMG LV](https://kpmg.com/lv/lv/petijumi-un-publikacijas/2025/09/izmainas-likuma-par-nodokliem-nodevam.html)
- **Shadow economy, SSE Riga index 2025:** LV 21.8 % of GDP, of which 47.1 % is envelope wages. [LSM 15.6.2026](https://eng.lsm.lv/article/economy/economy/15.06.2026-latvias-shadow-economy-grew-last-year-key-study-suggests.a651471/)
- **VDI:** 52 roadside transport checks with police in 2023. [VDI](https://vdi.gov.lv/lv/media/2592/download?attachment=)

### 4.2 How it appears on paper
- **[M] Union** (Ü. Kallas, ERR 2/2026): a large share of long-haul pay is per diem, which doesn't count toward pension or sick pay. Employers often claim damages from drivers.   - Domestic trips have no tax-free per diem.
  - The driver is sent either on an Estonian work trip or as a posted worker. A1 certificate per driver.
  - [emta.ee](https://www.emta.ee/ariklient/e-teenused-koolitused/nouanded/kaugsoiduautojuhi-lahetamine)
- **[V] Rates 2026:** income tax 22 % (the planned 24 % was cancelled), flat 700 €/month tax-free allowance. Social tax 33 %, minimum base 886 € (292.38 €/month). Unemployment insurance 1.6 % employee / 0.8 % employer. II pillar 2/4/6 %. [EMTA 2026](https://emta.ee/uudised/maksumuudatused-2026)

[toostusuudised](https://www.toostusuudised.ee/uudised/2026/10/30/valitsus-tostab-valistoojou-kvoodi-kolmekordseks), [Autoettevõtete Liit](https://autoettevoteteliit.ee/juhtide-puudus-viib-transpordi-kriis/)
- **[U] Gaps:** OÜ (contractor) drivers and agency setups.

### 4.3 Enforcement and risk [V]
- **Supreme Court:**
  - 3-2-1-187-13: per diem is **not wage** (it doesn't count toward the minimum wage).
  - 3-3-1-86-10: if Estonia is named as the workplace only for tax optimisation, per diems are wage.
  - 3-2-1-80-15: no genuine work trip → per diem payments are wage income.
  - Claims for unpaid per diem and wage interest: 3-year limitation.
  - [toooigusabi](https://www.toooigusabi.ee/riigikohtu-5-marts-2014-otsus-kohtuasjas-3-2-1-187-13-est), [toooigusabi 80-15](https://toooigusabi.ee/riigikohtu-21september-2015-otsus-kohtuasjas-3-2-1-80-15-est), [postimees](https://www.postimees.ee/7450438/riigikohus-tootasu-viivist-ja-paevaraha-saab-nouda-kolm-aastat)
- **EMTA risk model.** A firm whose 12-month average pay is below the average for the same positions gets flagged. The worker is jointly liable for income tax on envelope pay. About 25 % of firms are involved; 15 700 firms are rated "red". Reassessment 3 years back. Interest 0.06 %/day. [EMTA](https://www.emta.ee/uudised/mta-risk-umbrikupalga-maksmisega-vahele-jaada-suur), [EMTA interest](https://emta.ee/ariklient/maksud-ja-tasumine/tasumine-volad/intresside-tasumine)
- **Shadow economy, SSE Riga index 2025:** EE 20.8 % of GDP (highest since 2009), of which 43.7 % is envelope wages.

## 6. Calculator design: agreed pay models, inputs and outputs

### 6.1 Agreed pay models (driver side: "what is owed under the agreement")
| Code | Agreement | Inputs | Typical defaults (editable, from §1–4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DAY_NET` | Net per day | day rate (currency); **which days count**: days on route / all days of the rotation / working days only; weekend abroad paid (yes/no or a separate rate); waiting days (full / partial / 0); probation rate + weeks | PL 400–500 PLN · LT 90–120 € · LV 65–90 € · EE 80–115 € |
| `DAY_NET_BY_COUNTRY` | Day rate by work country/region (e.g. NL 120, DE 110, Baltics 90) | rate table per country + country segments | LT/LV ads |
| `BASE_PLUS_DAY` | Gross (or net) base per month + allowance per day abroad | base, per-day amount + currency, days abroad, FX | PL 5 500 PLN + 50–62.5 EUR |
| `MONTHLY_NET` | Fixed net per month (often for a rotation month) | amount, rotation, pro rata rule for partial months | PL 7.5–11 k PLN · LT/LV/EE 2 000–3 500 € |
| `PER_TRIP` | Per round trip / circuit | price per trip type, trips | LV "per trip ~2 000 €" |
| `PER_KM` (bonus or full) | Rate per km loaded / empty | km loaded, km empty, two rates | PL 0.50–0.80 PLN/km on top of a base |
| `PCT_FREIGHT` | % of freight revenue | freight revenue, % | owner-drivers/subcontractors; bonus only for employees |
| `TIME_TES` / `PIECE_TES` | FI/NO/DK/SE agreement pay: hourly, or piece rate with a guaranteed hourly floor | hours, piece units, rates | FI TES (piece target +30 %) |
| `CONTRACTOR` | B2B invoice per day/km | as above, shown as cost | flag: misclassification risk |

**Modifiers for every model:**
- **Rotation** (2/1, 3/1, 4/1, 4/2, 6/2, 6/3, 8/4 weeks).
- **Bonuses:** fuel economy (target l/100 km vs actual), no damage, on time, ADR, seniority, double crew.
- **Deductions:** fines, damage, fuel overuse, lodging, administration, recruiter fee. Each has a "basis" field (written consent / court / none). The calculator shows legality warnings: PL KP art. 119/282 (3-month cap, written consent); LT Res. 518 (no per diem cuts as a penalty); minimum-wage protection.
- **Advances** already paid (a field only; no tracking of cash flows outside payroll).

### 6.2 Legal comparison (computed from seed-data.json; same days and hours)
1. **Legal minimum gross** for the period: national minimum (pro rata) + night / overtime / holiday supplements from the hours worked.
2. **Host minimum on posted days** (cabotage / cross-trade): hours × posting minimum (DE 13.90, FR 12.31, NL 14.99, NO 244.50 NOK, DK 196.08 DKK, FI TES). Per diems are excluded unless the contract labels a part as wage.
3. **Statutory allowance per day:**
   - PL: 20 EUR PIT / 60 EUR ZUS reliefs, ZUS floor 9 420 PLN.
   - LT: Res. 526 norm per country; tax-free only if base ≥ 1 902.45 €, otherwise ≤ 50 % of wage; Res. 518 floor of 65 %.
   - LV: MK 969 rates, 70 % cap.
   - EE: 75 € (first 15 days) / 40 €.
4. **Gap analysis.** Agreed net minus net of the lawful structure (minimum-compliant base + per diem within norms) = **amount that must be paid as taxable wage**. The calculator shows the gross-up and employer cost for it.
5. **Third-country permit floor:** LT 2 893 € (post-quota permits from 9/2026), LV 1 637 € (sector average for 2024). Warn if the declared base is below it.
6. **Exposure if the paper version differs** (indicative only; shows the gross-up cost of the excess, plus interest per year of delay):

### 6.3 Not modelled (by design)
How to hide income; tracking cash paid off the books; "optimal" splits for avoiding tax. The tool shows what the agreement owes, the lawful structure that delivers it, and the cost of that structure.
